The other half of the moon

Zoha,

Happy birthday. I wanted today to be simple for you, and I know it isn’t completely. Our families aren’t where we want them to be. Some days it feels like we’re standing on two hills with all of it sitting in the valley between us.

So I keep coming back to the moon on your birthday. Half of it is lit and half of it is dark, and nobody looks up and thinks the moon is broken. Everyone knows the rest of it is there. It’s whole. The light just hasn’t reached that side yet.

That’s how I see us. Some of what we have, the people around us can’t see yet. It’s still whole. I don’t wonder whether it’s real. And the light is already on its way.

Did you see the little lights between the two houses? They started from both ends and met in the middle. That’s how I want this to happen. With respect for your family and for mine, from both sides, until we meet in the middle.

But today isn’t about any of that. Today is about you. You are the kindest, bravest, most beautiful person I know, and the world got lucky the day you arrived in it. I’m so glad you were born. I’m so glad it’s you.

Happy birthday, my love.Kaleem

P.S. Your sunflower field is still standing, exactly where you left it. Go and visit it. And I still owe you the real ones.
