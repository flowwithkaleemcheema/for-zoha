Sunrise · the rest of it

Everything the flowers couldn’t hold

Zoha,

Busy is a reason. It isn’t a good enough one. You don’t get whatever’s left of me after everything else got loud. That’s not the deal, and I let it become the deal anyway.

I couldn’t get sunflowers to your door this week, and that bothered me more than it should have, it’s such a small thing to get right. So I sat down and made you these instead. They took hours. That was the point. Time is the thing I owe you, so I spent some of it here.

I’m not asking you to be fine yet. Be annoyed with me as long as you need to be; you’ve earned it. I’ll be here the whole time, facing your direction.

I am sorry. I love you more than anything I have ever loved. We will be together and then I will send you the real flowers.

— Kaleem Cheema

P.S. Your field saves itself. Close this, come back next week, and every flower you planted will still be standing exactly where you put it.
